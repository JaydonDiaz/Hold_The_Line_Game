# Hold the Line

A castle-defence game. Hire Knights, Archers, Mages, Frost Witches and more, post them across the field, and hold the line against waves of goblins, orcs, trolls, slimes and worse. Unite the peoples of the continent in the campaign, or play Classic, Freeplay and the Battle Modes on their own.

## Play

- **In your browser:** https://jaydondiaz.github.io/Hold_The_Line_Game/
- **Windows:** download [`downloads/HoldTheLine-windows.zip`](downloads/HoldTheLine-windows.zip), unzip it and run `HoldTheLine.exe`. If Windows SmartScreen warns about an unknown app, choose *More info → Run anyway*.
- **Mac:** download [`downloads/HoldTheLine-mac.zip`](downloads/HoldTheLine-mac.zip) and unzip it. The app isn't signed, so the first time, right-click it and choose *Open*, then *Open* again.

## Controls

- Pick a unit from the panel and click the field to post it there. Click a unit to select it, then upgrade it, give it orders or sell it.
- Right-click to send the selected unit somewhere during a wave.
- The panel's buttons show their keyboard shortcuts in brackets, such as [S] to sell.
